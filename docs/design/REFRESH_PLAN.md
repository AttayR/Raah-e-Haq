## 1. Why it doesn't feel production-grade (diagnosis)

The palette, type scale and grid from 2026-10-08 are fine and are already in `src/theme` (T-601 done). The problem is application:

1. **Two design generations ship side by side.** Screens rebuilt on `src/components/ui` (passenger Home, driver Home/Settings, Settings v2, Inbox, chat, documents) sit next to legacy screens on `BrandColors` with navy bubble heroes and the illustrated `ImageBackground` wallpaper (Login, Phone verification, Registration, both Profiles, legacy passenger Settings). 24 files still import `BrandColors`; 6 use `ImageBackground`; `DriverProfile.tsx` alone has 58 hex literals. Moving between tabs feels like moving between two apps. → `auth-login-light.png`, `auth-phone-light.png`, `driver-profile-light.png` vs `driver-settings-light.png`.
2. **Dark mode breaks on every legacy screen.** Wallet, Ride history, Saved places, legacy Profile, Login, Phone and the booking sheet's vehicle cards and fare card stay white in dark mode; Registration and Phone show black inputs on white cards. → `passenger-wallet-dark.png`, `passenger-history-dark.png`, `passenger-vehicle-dark.png`, `passenger-fare-dark.png`, `auth-phone-dark.png`, `driver-profile-dark.png`.
3. **The primary action is not obvious and not on-brand.** Booking's CTA is a periwinkle "🔔 Confirm & Request", prices are periwinkle, vehicle tiles are emoji on navy squares; driver Accept is green; passenger Cancel is a pink slab; Login shows three different button blues. → `passenger-fare-light.png`, `passenger-vehicle-light.png`, `ref-driver-incoming-request-light.png`, `ref-passenger-driver-assigned.png`.
4. **The map layer is default Google.** Teardrop pins (green/blue), no route casing, four unlabeled floating circles (back, locate, refresh, +), an opaque grey band over the status bar on the driver map, a red power FAB. Top apps win on the map; ours looks like a demo. → `passenger-booking-light.png`, `driver-map-light.png`.
5. **The booking flow reads like a developer stepper.** "1. Pickup · 2. Destination · 3. Vehicle · 4. Fare" chips, no sticky CTA on the vehicle list, "Estimated fare" collides with the sheet handle, the back button walks through half-cleared states with the route still drawn. → `passenger-booking-light.png`, `passenger-vehicle-scrolled-light.png`, `passenger-booking-backstep-dark.png`.
6. **Roles are out of sync.** Passenger has 4 tabs, driver 5 (Home and Map duplicate each other); the driver trip sheet uses a Call/Message/Navigate icon trio while the passenger sheet uses a full-width Call inside the card plus stacked Message and Cancel slabs; driver prints `PKR 150`, passenger `Rs 150`; the incoming request floats over the Home dashboard rather than the map. → `driver-home-light.png` + `driver-map-light.png`, `ref-driver-ride-to-pickup-light.png` vs `ref-passenger-driver-assigned.png`.
7. **Four header styles and two back glyphs.** Large left title (tab roots), centered title + chevron under a 48pt blank band (Wallet, History, Saved places), left title + arrow (Profile v2, Chat, Documents), navy bubble hero (legacy Profile, auth). → `passenger-wallet-light.png`, `passenger-profile-v2-dark.png`, `passenger-profile-legacy-light.png`.
8. **Icons and identity glitches.** 13 icon families in code (Icon.tsx itself on MaterialIcons because the MDI font isn't bundled); "?" glyphs (Saved places "Work", driver Profile "Earnings"); solid-colour avatar blobs with no initials (passenger chat list, driver avatar); grey silhouette placeholders; hardcoded four stars. → `passenger-saved-places-light.png`, `passenger-chat-list-light.png`, `driver-settings-light.png`.
9. **Stale demo content and Title Case copy.** Wallet "Rs 1200" with Add Funds/Payment Methods (cash only) and September 2025 transactions, History cards from September 2025 with a navy "Details" slab on each, driver Profile "156 rides / $2.4k"; Title Case everywhere ("Ride History", "Favorite Locations", "Sign Out", notification titles); brand spelled "RaaH-E-Haq". → `passenger-wallet-light.png`, `passenger-history-light.png`, `driver-profile-light.png`.
10. **No feel layer.** No haptics anywhere in `src/`, product confirmations use native `Alert.alert` (11 files), no skeletons, empty states are stray sentences or dashes ("Today's numbers aren't available yet."). → `passenger-home-light.png`, `driver-home-light.png`.

What is already good and must be kept: Settings v2 layout (both roles), notifications list structure, chat thread bubbles and closed-chat footer, Vehicle & documents list, driver Home online card, the custom map style in dark mode, bilingual map labels.

## 2. Competitive bar (principles to match, not branding to copy)

| Competitor | What they do well | What we must match (and where) |
|---|---|---|
| **Uber** | Ruthless hierarchy: one primary action per state, large status sentence as the sheet title, monochrome chrome so the map and the CTA stand out; vehicle list with ETA + price + capacity and a CTA that names the product; plate and car prominent on the driver card; route with casing and clear pickup/dropoff glyphs. | Map archetype with one sheet-footer CTA (§12.2) · "Choose Rickshaw · Rs 104" CTA (UI-R10) · status-sentence titles + progress bar (UI-R12/13) · custom pins + cased route (UI-R5) · plate pill on PersonCard (UI-R5). |
| **Careem** | Warm, local and friendly; greeting by time of day; saved places as one-tap shortcuts on Home; cash-first, plain-language money; bilingual readiness; rounded pill buttons. | Greeting + "Where to?" + Home/Work shortcut chips on the map-first Home (UI-R4, UI-R9) · pill buttons (UI-R2) · `Rs 1,250` everywhere, "Pay cash to your driver" copy (UI-R1 formatters) · i18n/RTL scaffold (UI-R1). |
| **inDrive** | Driver efficiency and fare transparency: request card leads with the price, then pickup distance/ETA, big Accept, visible countdown; high-contrast flat UI readable in sunlight. | Incoming request sheet: fare first, countdown ring on Accept, 56pt buttons, 20pt addresses (UI-R11) · fare breakdown visible before requesting (UI-R10) · AA contrast in both modes (every task). |
| **Yango** | Map-first from the first second: the Home sheet *is* the search; recent destinations as chips; smooth animated car markers that rotate with heading; polished dark mode; snappy sheet springs. | Map-first Home with sheet (UI-R4 structure, UI-R9 content) · CarMarker interpolation + rotation and searching pulse (UI-R5) · sheet spring + content swap motion (UI-R3, DS §6.12) · dark mode that never falls back to white cards (every task). |
| **All four** | Consistency: the same components on every screen, real empty states, skeleton loading, haptic confirmation on commits, 44pt+ targets. | Kit and shell first (UI-R1…R5), EmptyState/Skeleton/ErrorState (UI-R3), haptics map (DS §6.11), legacy sweep gate (UI-R19). |
